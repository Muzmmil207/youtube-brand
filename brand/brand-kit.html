Thumbnails

The formula

[Outcome] + [Friction removed]

“Any theme. Zero code.” · “Sell more. Same traffic.” · “Faster checkout. One app.”

  • Two lines, two to three words each. Never a sentence.
  • One word in lime — the one that carries the promise.
  • Headline 90–110px on the 1280 × 720 canvas.
  • Category pill top-left, episode tag top-right, wordmark bottom-left.
  • Keep the bottom-right corner clear — YouTube puts the duration there.

Do

  • Same layout every episode — recognition beats novelty.
  • Show the actual screen you'll teach on, abstracted to bars.
  • Check it at 168px wide before you publish.

Don't

  • Red arrows, shocked faces, yellow circles.
  • More than one accent colour.
  • Text under 24px or over 4 words per line.

Voice

Plain

Name the click, not the concept. “Products → Add product”, not “catalogue management”.

Practical

Every video ends with the store in a better state than it started.

Unhyped

No “insane”, no “secret”. A merchant's time is the thing you're protecting.
